# North stars

These are the practices we take from people whose work has earned trust. Each one is tracked the way a [Thoughtworks Technology Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/radar/byor) tracks technologies:

- **Adopt:** proven here; the default.
- **Trial:** in use and being measured.
- **Assess:** worth a look; not in use yet.
- **Hold:** looked at and set aside, with the reason.

Nothing here is gospel. An entry earns its place by helping the project, and leaves when it stops doing that.

**What makes a source credible.** Named practitioners with years of practice behind their writing: books, long-running blogs, or tools that people actually use. Volume of posting doesn't count. Ryan's own [on-writing-code](https://github.com/hartz89/on-writing-code) is a source too, under the same rules. It is expected to change because of what this project learns.

**How an entry moves.** An entry moves from Trial to Adopt when there's evidence: a metric moved, a class of bug stopped, or the process held up for a month of real use. Anything that reaches Adopt and isn't specific to Docent is a candidate for on-writing-code (code and process) or bonsai (harness mechanics). Mark it in the **Export** column, then open a PR there.
